📊 HR Analytics Dashboard

📌 Project Overview

The HR Analytics Dashboard provides an interactive and data-driven view of employee demographics, workforce distribution, and employee attrition. It helps HR professionals understand workforce trends, identify attrition patterns, and make informed, data-driven decisions.

The dashboard presents key HR metrics through interactive visualizations, charts, and comparative analysis.

🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze employee attrition and retention trends.
- Understand employee distribution across departments and age groups.
- Compare attrition patterns by gender and education field.
- Explore job satisfaction ratings across different job roles.
- Present key HR metrics in a clear, interactive dashboard.

📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

KPI| Value
Total Employees| 1,470
Attrition Count| 237
Attrition Rate| 16.12%
Active Employees| 1,233
Average Employee Age| 37

📊 Dashboard Features

1. Department-Wise Attrition

Visualizes employee attrition across HR, Research & Development, and Sales departments.

2. Employee Age Group Analysis

Displays workforce distribution across different age groups to understand employee demographics.

3. Job Satisfaction Analysis

Compares job satisfaction ratings across various job roles.

4. Education Field-Wise Attrition

Analyzes attrition across educational backgrounds, including Life Sciences, Medical, Marketing, Technical Degree, and Human Resources.

5. Gender-Based Attrition Analysis

Examines attrition patterns by gender across different age groups.

6. Interactive Filtering

Includes an Education Field filter to explore workforce metrics for selected educational categories.

🔍 Key Insights

- The dashboard reports an overall attrition rate of 16.12%.
- Out of 1,470 employees, 237 employees are recorded under attrition, while 1,233 remain active.
- The average employee age is 37 years.
- Sales accounts for the largest share of department-wise attrition in the displayed analysis.
- Life Sciences and Medical are the leading education fields by attrition count in the dashboard.

These observations describe the displayed dashboard and do not establish the causes of employee attrition.
